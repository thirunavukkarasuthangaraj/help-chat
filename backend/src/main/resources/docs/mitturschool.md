# GHSS Mittur help documentation

## Where is the school located? What is the address?
GHSS Mittur (Government Higher Secondary School) is located at Mittur Village, Tirupattur District, Tamil Nadu 635710. You can find the map and directions on our Contact page: https://mitturschool.com/contact.html

## How do I contact the school? What is the phone number?
You can reach GHSS Mittur by phone at 6374217724. You can also visit the Contact page for the address, visiting hours, and location map: https://mitturschool.com/contact.html

## How do I apply for admission? Admission enquiry
For admission enquiries, please contact the school office by phone at 6374217724 or visit the school at Mittur Village, Tamil Nadu 635710 during working hours. Being a government school, admission is open to all eligible students. Details: https://mitturschool.com/contact.html

## What classes and courses are offered?
GHSS Mittur is a Government Higher Secondary School serving students up to higher secondary level (Plus Two). For the current list of groups and subjects offered, please contact the school office at 6374217724.

## What facilities does the school have?
The school provides classrooms, library, playground, and other student facilities. See the full list with photos on our Facilities page: https://mitturschool.com/facilities.html

## Who are the teachers? Staff details
You can see our teaching staff on the Teachers page: https://mitturschool.com/teachers.html

## What events and activities happen at school?
The school conducts events like Republic Day celebrations, NCC and NSS activities, medical camps, and career guidance programmes. See photos and updates on the Events page: https://mitturschool.com/events.html

## What is the history of the school?
GHSS Mittur has been serving the Mittur village community for many years as a government school. Read the full story on our History page: https://mitturschool.com/history.html

## What are the school working hours / timings?
For current school timings and office visiting hours, please call the school office at 6374217724 or check the Contact page: https://mitturschool.com/contact.html
