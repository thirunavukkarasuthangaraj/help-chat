# Demo App Help Documentation

## Getting started
To get started with Demo App, create an account using your work email, verify the email link, and complete your profile. After signing in you will land on the Dashboard, which shows your recent activity and quick actions.

## Reset your password
To reset your password, click "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. Enter your registered email and we will send a reset link. The link expires in 30 minutes. If you do not receive the email, check your spam folder or contact support.

## Pricing plans
Demo App offers three plans. Free: 1 user, basic features. Pro: 25 US dollars per user per month, includes reporting and integrations. Enterprise: custom pricing with SSO, audit logs, and dedicated support. You can upgrade or downgrade any time from Settings, then Billing.

## Invite team members
Go to Settings, then Team, then Invite. Enter email addresses separated by commas and choose a role: Admin, Member, or Viewer. Invitees receive an email and must accept within 7 days.

## Notifications
You can manage notifications from Settings, then Notifications. Email, in-app, and mobile push channels can each be turned on or off per event type such as mentions, task assignments, and weekly summaries.

## Contact support
Email support@demoapp.example or use the Help menu, then Contact us. Support hours are Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM US Eastern. Enterprise customers have 24/7 support.
